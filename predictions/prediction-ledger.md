# FPM Prediction Ledger 2026

The public ledger of falsifiable predictions published by *First Principles Manufacturing*.

---

## Operating Rules of the Ledger

1. **Strictly Public Claims**: Only falsifiable predictions explicitly published in *First Principles Manufacturing* essays are entered here.
2. **Explicit Verification Conditions**: Every entry defines the operational test condition, measurable threshold, and review cadence.
3. **Public Reckoning**: Quarterly reviews track ongoing signals. Every December, an annual audit essay is published detailing which forecasts held, which failed, and why. Public accountability on errors is the publication's primary credibility asset.
